# Washington Student Debt Navigation

A Washington public-interest project about student retention, consumer debt litigation, garnishment, and the handoff between campus student-support systems and qualified legal help.

The project grew from Jesse Zesbaugh's experience as an AmeriCorps Benefits Hub Coach and as a student who carried a consumer judgment through school. It is designed for several audiences: students seeking help, Benefits Hub coaches and Basic Needs Navigators, college leaders, legislators, regulators, financial partners, and the public.

## Core question

Washington has built systems to help students navigate food, housing, financial aid, emergency expenses, and other barriers to staying enrolled. Legal resources for consumer debt also exist.

**The resources exist. The handoff is not automatic.**

This project asks what happens when an enrolled student receives a debt-collection summons, judgment, or garnishment notice—and whether the systems already designed to keep that student in school know how to connect them to qualified legal help.

## Important distinctions

- Sam, Maya, and Priya are hypothetical.
- Jesse's case is presented separately and is based on his records.
- The project does not advocate debt forgiveness or claim that all students experience the same outcome.
- The website provides general public information, not legal advice.

## Sources and review

Legal, institutional, and statistical claims are checked against primary or authoritative sources and are cited on the site. Because laws and resources change, see the visible **Last reviewed** date on the Sources page.

## AI transparency

AI tools assisted with research organization, drafting, editing, and some illustrations. AI output is not treated as a factual source. Human review determines what is published.
